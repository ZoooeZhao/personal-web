Are People Open To Flocker?

We conducted surveys to understand current trends and preferences in event discovery, social interaction, and technology that fosters real-world connections. Youth were the primary audience, with participants from other age groups also recruited.

How often do you typically meet up with friends in person?

Frequent outings signal a sizable market for events.

How do you typically find out about events you are interested in?

People rely on social media to find events.

Would you go out more often if you could find potential or existing friends already doing things that match your interests?

Having a companion encourages more frequent outings.

Would you like to see what events your friends are interested in or are planning to attend?

There is a need for friend attendance information.

Key Insights

  • 53% have a hard time finding events.
  • 54% find it challenging to find friends to accompany them to events.
  • 90% want an app that suggests potential friends based on shared interests or mutual connections.
  • 93% would go out more often if they could find someone to attend an event with.
Isolation stems not from a lack of events, but from not having someone to go with.

People are reluctant to attend events because finding companions is difficult, a challenge compounded by technology-induced isolation. Flocker bridges this gap by helping people easily find, create, and attend pick-up games, concerts, and casual hangouts.

Relevant Events, Without The Overwhelm

The challenge was to help users discover relevant events without being overwhelmed by too many options or irrelevant suggestions.

How Can We Tailor Event Discovery To Each User’s Preferences?

A recommendation system personalizes content based on preferences, past behavior, and location. Research led the team to address this challenge from three perspectives.

Swipable Event Cards

An intuitive, AI-powered experience analyzes swipes in real time. The gamified interaction engages users while continuously refining event suggestions based on their preferences.

Sign-Up Interests

When someone first joins, the AI has no data about their event preferences. Understanding initial interests allows Flocker to recommend relevant events and personalize the experience from the start.

Mood-Based Recommendations

Traditional event-type categories created too many choices and were ineffective. Users preferred searching by keywords, leaving category browsing underused. Recommendations based on a user’s current mood simplify the categories, cover a broader range of events, and improve usability and engagement.

From Discovery To Showing Up

The core experience connects personalized event discovery with planning, attendance, and the people who make an event worth going to.

Swipable Event Cards

AI suggests relevant friends and events to create or attend, based on history and the social graph.

  • Only the essential event information is shown, reducing distraction and overwhelm.
  • Location and date tags help users find specific events.
  • User moods are displayed, and recommendations respond to them.
  • Swiping right means “interested” by default; swipe and select to RSVP.
Design Iterations ＋
  • Earlier cards displayed too much information, which distracted and overwhelmed users.
  • Keep the profile accessible at any time.
  • Revamp the UI for a cleaner look.

Event Browsing

Event planning made easy: see where future crowds will flock at a glance.

  • Popular filters provide quick access.
  • A map view helps users identify event locations and popular gathering spots.
  • Heatmap bubbles show user activity.
  • Zoom in to see specific events, color-coded by category.
Design Iterations ＋
  • Explore different display options for event browsing.
  • Replace the drawer with a floating mini event card.
  • Allow zooming to specific events and use color to distinguish categories.

In-App Calendar

Manage your day and stay updated with the weather, all within the app.

  • Check the weather alongside the calendar to plan the day.
  • Color-coded dots distinguish event types.
  • Open an event’s details directly from the calendar.
Design Iterations ＋
  • Change the calendar background to white for a simpler look.
  • Explore different calendar layouts and event-detail views.

Easy Event Management

Organize and engage with participants, and connect with people who share common interests.

  • Use consistent swipe interactions throughout the app.
  • Toggle between list and card views to manage attendees.
  • Profile cards show basic information and personal characteristics.
  • Keep hosts informed about event capacity.
Design Iterations ＋
  • View all attendees and explore attendee matching.
  • Explore different card designs.
